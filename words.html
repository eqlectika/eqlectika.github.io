War Of The Words — Terminal

Circle — single tap shuffles unhighlighted words, long tap opens the caret to add word or phrase, drag to move.

Input pop-up — enter a word and click "Add" or tap Enter; the nearest word or phrase will be added before or after by the entered text with author Peer-XXXX or name caption, highlighted in red. This is the only edit that is automatically synced to other devices where the app is open at the same time as yours (Live mode).

In Live mode caret edits are sent to all peers immediately. In Tape mode they're stored locally and flushed when a peer appears.

Word taps (local only): fresh → white → green → orange → open ChatGPT definition (and copy to clipboard). Long tap cancels highlight, or deletes the word if it wasn't highlighted. These changes stay on your device — other peers are not notified.

📎 Media button in the popover opens a file picker. Selected image or video is added to your local grid, scaled automatically to fit alongside words. Only the author's name appears above media. Tap any media to open full-screen viewer; tap again to close. The popover accepts either a word OR a media file — not both at once.

Input field below adds words to your device only. They are not broadcast to other peers.

Shuffle is local only and affects only non-highlighted words.

SYNC (red button) appears only when there are edits waiting in Tape. Tap it to force-send them once a peer is online.

Your peer name — tap the ID in the top-right corner, type a new name, tap Enter. 3–12 chars: letters, digits, dash, underscore. Escape or tap away to cancel.
