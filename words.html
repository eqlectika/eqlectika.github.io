War Of The Words — Terminal

Circle — single tap shuffles unhighlighted words. Long tap opens the caret (add word). Drag to move.

Caret popover — type a word, tap Add. It is inserted before/after the nearest word as red fresh text. This is the only edit that is sent to other devices (in Live mode).

Word taps (local only): fresh → white → green → orange → open ChatGPT definition (and copy to clipboard). Long tap cancels highlight, or deletes the word if it wasn't highlighted. These changes stay on your device — other peers are not notified.

Input field below adds words to your device only. They are not broadcast to other peers.

In Live mode caret edits are sent to all peers immediately. In Tape mode they're stored locally and flushed when a peer appears.

SYNC appears only when there are edits waiting in Tape. Tap it to force-send them once a peer is online.

Shuffle is local only and affects only non-highlighted words.

Your peer name — tap the ID in the top-right corner, type a new name, press Enter. 3–12 chars: letters, digits, dash, underscore. Escape or tap away to cancel.
