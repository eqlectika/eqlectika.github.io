War Of The Words

First tap highlights the word green. Second tap highlights it orange. Third tap opens request for definition via ChatGPT.

Long tap removes word and highlight.

External operating system or may retain a previous word for reasons beyond this app's control.

Currently selected word will be copied to the clipboard anytime, so you can paste it instead if an older word appears in the request.

For convenience and to protect your data, add this page to your Home Screen via the browser menu and use that shortcut to launch it.
