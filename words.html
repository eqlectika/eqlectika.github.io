War Of The Words — Terminal

Circle — single tap shuffles unhighlighted words. Long tap opens the caret (add word). Drag to move.

Caret popover — type a word, tap Add. It is inserted before/after the nearest word as red fresh text.

Word taps: fresh > white > green > orange > open ChatGPT definition (and copy to clipboard). Long tap deletes the word.

In Live mode edits are sent to all peers immediately. In Tape mode they're stored locally and flushed when a peer appears (or via the Sync button).

Shuffle is local only and affects only non-highlighted words.

Your peer name — tap the ID in the top-right corner, type a new name, press Enter. 3–12 chars: letters, digits, dash, underscore. Escape or tap away to cancel.
