About

Resonance Match: Tap one meme to elevate its ranking, or other to drop opponent.

To rise, vote for the weak memes against the strong ones.

Common votes are counted only in a live session.

Global Matrix Rank: The ranking engine calculates the relative strength of players using the Elo formula.

Broadcast Breakthrough Meme: The meme you publish will appear and be stored only in the cache of devices of users who are currently live session. No one will see your meme if it is injected (posted) in tape mode (offline). However, you can always Re-inject your tape mode meme (free) directly from ranking via Edit... button.

State and Credits: Every competitor gets 8 free impacts, 5 free votes and 3 free publication slots.

Hourly Resonance Claim: lets you earn +4 Impact per hour.

If you return in a day (or later), the long-term absence limitation will kick in, and you will receive +24 impact.

You can delete any meme in the ranking.

Use 'Convert Impacts to Action Limits' to spend 1 impact for +3 votes, or 3 impacts for +1 post slot.

Use 'Transfer Impact(s)' to send it(s) to other network participants.

Due to the temporary absence of individual participants and the presence of memes they cannot see, the number of votes required to update ranking does not accumulate synchronously across devices. When live parity is reached, "spontaneous" updates of the pair in the voting block occur.
