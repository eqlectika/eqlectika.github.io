Steam — Map of the words

Single tap an empty or your own cell — opens the input popup with the keyboard.

Single tap someone else's cell — adds a flag (one flag per user per cell).

Double tap any occupied cell — action menu: flag / request field / open ChatGPT for definition at interface language.

Long tap someone else's cell — request the field.

Long tap your own cell — free it; the word goes to history with author and flag count.

Live = edits broadcast immediately. Tape = edits queued locally.

Tap the peer ID top-right to rename yourself (3–9 chars, letters/digits/-/_).
