Auction Logic

Any ranking item is turned into an auction in the Broadcast panel below. If you want to place bid as a buyer, you need to make a symbolic offer. Offers & bids are accepted while both participants is LIVE online; when the seller goes offline the auction switches to TAPE (saved bids only).
