About

Resonance Match: Tap one item to elevate its ranking, or other to drop opponent.

To rise, vote for the weak items against the strong ones.

Common votes are counted only in a live session.

Global Matrix Rank: The ranking engine calculates the relative strength of players using the Elo formula.

Broadcast Breakthrough Item: The item you publish will appear and be stored only in the cache of devices of users who are currently live session. No one will see your item if it is injected (posted) in tape mode (offline). However, you can always Re-inject your tape mode item (free) directly from ranking via Edit... button.

State and Credits: Every competitor gets 8 free impacts, 5 free votes and 3 free publication slots.

Hourly Resonance Claim: lets you earn +4 Impact per hour.

If you return in a day (or later), the long-term absence limitation will kick in, and you will receive +24 impact.

You can delete any item in the ranking.

Use 'Convert Impacts to Action Limits' to spend 1 impact for +3 votes, or 3 impacts for +1 post slot.

Use 'Transfer Impact(s)' to send it(s) to other network participants.

Auction Logic: Any ranking-item can be turned into an auction in the Broadcast panel below. The author of the item is always the seller — no external seller can be assigned. Only the starting bid and an optional Buy Now price are set. Bids are accepted while the seller is LIVE; when the seller goes offline the auction switches to TAPE (saved bids only). Bids are accepted 24/7 without fixed time windows — the LIVE/TAPE state of the seller is the only gate.

Seller LIVE: Buyer can press Bid — the bid is really sent to the seller, who may Accept it. After acceptance the deal gets status Delivered and a private gate opens between the two peers for additional non-public info. Buy Now (if set) instantly closes the deal at the seller's price.

Seller TAPE (offline): Buyer can only view the auction data received while the seller was live. New bids are stored as Save (intended bids). Buy Now is unavailable. When the seller becomes LIVE again, the buyer is offered to edit and/or re-bid — same pattern as edit/re-inject for items.

⚠️ Live peer exchange of additional information works only if both participants are online right now. If either party goes offline, the gate is closed and messages sent while the peer is offline are not delivered until they return LIVE.

Due to the temporary absence of individual participants and the presence of items they cannot see, the number of votes required to update ranking does not accumulate synchronously across devices. When live parity is reached, "spontaneous" updates of the pair in the voting block occur.
