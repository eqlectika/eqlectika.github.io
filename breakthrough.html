Concept & Tips

'Broadcast Breakthrough Vector': The ranking engine calculates the relative strength of players using the ELO formula.

Add 2-3 images (vector) to see how it works.

Then, to promote the last meme in the ranking, click on the large equivalents above.

Other participants can see your meme vector and vote for them.

You can also vote for other people's meme(s) that may appear on your screen.

If no one is online, your meme(s) can not be visible by anyone (so share the link with someone to interact).

Every competitor gets 8 free impacts, 5 free votes and 3 free publication slots.

'Hourly Resonance Claim': lets you earn +4 Impact per hour

(theoretically, would accumulate 96 impacts per 24 hours).

If you return in a day (or later), the long-term absence limitation will kick in, and you will receive only 24 impact.

Deleting a vector locally (click on meme in the ranking) incurs a penalty of -1 Impact.

Use 'Convert Impacts to Action Limits' to spend 1 impact for +3 votes, or 3 impacts for +1 post slot.

Use 'Transfer Impact(s)' to send it(s) to other network participants.

You 'Buy Impacts via MetaMask' wallet with Ethereum.

Install Metamask extension on your desktop browser and/or Metamask app on your mobile device and then follow instruction.

Boost your vector(s) by voting for it.

To rise, vote for the weak memes against the strong ones.

Due to the decentralized nature of interactions between participants, the number of votes for the same meme may vary across devices.

Moreover, there are memes between other participants you can not to see may be included in rotation.

Therefore, you may sometimes see unexpected changes in the meme rankings and, less commonly, in the voting block, which is a natural result of accumulating enough votes to cause a meme reshuffle on your device.
