Game Rules & Tips
  1. Every participant gets 8 free impacts, 5 free votes and 3 free publication slots.
  2. Use 'Convert Impacts to Action Limits' to spend 1 impact for +3 votes, or 3 impacts for +1 post slot.
  3. Hourly Resonance Claim lets you earn +1 Impact per hour (max +8 impacts per 24 hours).
  4. Deleting a vector locally incurs a penalty of -1 Impact.
  5. Use 'Transfer Impact' to send impacts to other network members.
