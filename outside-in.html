Controls & Rules

Touch & Reticle: Drag the reticle anywhere to aim. Tap opens the menu (Build, Attack, Hack); long-press the reticle (or hold/press F) to execute and continue building, attacking, or hacking.

Crossing the Surface: Fly through the boundary gates on R = 1500 or across the sphere wall to switch sides.

Houses & Stacking: Houses sit flush on the surface on your current side and stack strictly on top of existing roofs.

Own House Withdrawal & Centers: Withdraw stored tokens in Stats via Withdraw / Withdraw All. Architectural centers grow taller as nearby houses and stored tokens increase.
