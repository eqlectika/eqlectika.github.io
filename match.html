3D MineSweeper

Think in Depth

A true volumetric take on the classic puzzle.

The Twist

Every layer is its own unique battlefield, yet they're all connected — a single tap can ripple through empty spaces across multiple layers at once.

Mines are counted across all 26 neighbors in 3D space — including the layers above and below you. A 2 on your layer might mean both mines are on your plane… or one could be hiding one layer up.

Key Features

  • Unique Layers — each of the N layers has its own layout. No duplicates.
  • Layer Minimap — see all layers at a glance. Green layers are fully cleared. Tap to jump between them.
  • Smart Flood-Fill — opening an empty cell cascades through 3D space, revealing safe zones across multiple layers at once.
  • Scalable Cube — play on 5³, 8³, 13³, or 21³. Mines scale at 4% density for balanced difficulty.
  • Classic Controls — tap to reveal, double-tap (or right-click) to flag. The clock is ticking.
